Climate Monitoring

Dynamic Drought Module

Drought, heat, fire, and water conditions across North America, live from federal sources.

What it does

A public web map that draws from federal sources to show drought, heat, fire-weather, and water conditions across North America. Click a state, ecoregion, or boundary for a composed impact briefing covering four hazards across three time horizons.

Capabilities

  • RecordedCondition and event layers U.S. Drought Monitor, gridded SPI, seasonal outlooks, HeatRisk, fire perimeters, smoke plumes, and weather alerts.
  • RecordedStation readings USGS streamgages, NRCS SNOTEL, Reclamation Hydromet and AgriMet, and Army Corps reservoirs.
  • RecordedImpact briefing Click any state, ecoregion, or boundary for a composed briefing across four hazards and three time horizons.
  • RecordedShare and embed A link carries the full view; iframe embedding lets any site host the map.
  • RecordedDesktop 3D fire view A three-dimensional terrain view for active fire context (09/11/2026).

Toward 0.9.x

  • Verify standalone and integrated experiences for the 0.9.x candidate
What to know

Climate Hazards

CAST and SHIELD

Hazard modules with a phone-first dashboard designed for low-connectivity communities.

What it does

Climate Alert and Severity Tracker: a framework of hazard modules covering drought and heat, flood and coastal, severe weather, and snow and ice. SHIELD is its presentation layer, a location-aware, phone-first alert dashboard designed for low-connectivity communities and extending across shared watersheds to First Nations.

Capabilities

  • RecordedNormalized alert model A single model for alerts across agencies, with National Weather Service and Environment Canada parsers replayed against captured feeds.
  • RecordedHonest-status system Every source shows its condition (current, cached, stale, degraded, unavailable) with an 'as of' time.
  • RecordedVerified source registry Each data source is registered with its provenance tracked.
  • SyntheticSHIELD dashboard shell SHIELD's interface runs on fictional fixtures; it does not yet display current alerts.
Last recorded 07/17/2026
Phase 0.1 Foundation complete 07/17/2026; Phase 0.2 data layer begun, with no later record.
Toward 0.9.x
  • Scheduled alert publication and cross-agency alert grouping
  • Connect SHIELD to current alert feeds
  • Drought and Heat module via the DDM migration
What to know
SHIELD is the presentation layer, not a hazard engine; it shows no current alerts today.

Geospatial Planning

ATNI GeoBase

Local-first desktop map workstation for transparent renewable-energy siting.

What it does

A local-first desktop map workstation (fully offline, with zero network requests observed) for transparent renewable-energy siting. Hard exclusions, soft constraints, and a reason trace for every result; coordinate reference systems, custody, and provenance travel with the work. Unclassified data defaults to the Sovereign tier (T3) and is refused for export.

Capabilities

  • RecordedOffline runtime Zero network requests observed; the full application runs without an internet connection (08/11/2026).
  • RecordedLocal terrain and imagery Terrain and satellite imagery for Washington state, served from local files (09/10/2026).
  • RecordedSolar and wind resource layers Solar irradiation with a twelve-month scrubber and wind resource at three hub heights; no capacity claims (09/10/2026).
  • SyntheticExclusion zones and reason traces Every siting result carries a reason trace explaining which constraints applied (verified on synthetic data, 08/11/2026).
Last recorded 09/30/2026
0.1.0-development.0; sovereignty controls designed but not yet enforced. Partial verification on 09/30/2026.
Toward 0.9.x
  • Repair verification repeatability and reach the minimum sovereign map milestone
  • Enforce governance controls in the runtime
  • Projected TERRA role: shared geographic baseline and offline GeoPack for planners
What to know
Private development build; no public repository.

Policy Intelligence

Policy Sentinel

Policy monitoring workbench: passage search, version comparison, and custody receipts.

What it does

A sovereignty-centered policy monitoring and source-reference workbench. It watches bounded authoritative sources, explains why each record appears, keeps exact text changes between versions, and never implies a legal conclusion.

Capabilities

  • RecordedBill version comparison Compare versions of a bill with exact text differences preserved.
  • RecordedFindings with counterevidence Every finding surfaces counterevidence alongside the supporting passages.
  • RecordedReplayable custody receipts Every acquisition and processing step is recorded and replayable.

Offline tool that turns ATNI resolutions into a searchable, cited knowledge base.

What it does

An offline tool that turns ATNI's resolutions (1,114 listed, 2007 to June 2026) into a searchable, cited knowledge base. It quotes clauses verbatim with provenance and never paraphrases or generates resolution text.

Capabilities

  • PartialEvidence export DOCX export with clause citations; v1 behavior on a 19-resolution 2023 sample.
  • PlannedPrecedent brief Quotes prior resolutions word for word when drafting a new resolution.

Governance

Sovereignty by Design

Governance is architecture. Every layer of TERRA encodes a sovereignty decision; the Nation determines what moves, what stays, and what is refused.

  1. T0Open/Public Released by an affirmative sovereign act, never presumed.
  2. T1Network Shared among a trusted Indigenous network; views, not copies.
  3. T2Negotiated Named partners under a documented, revocable agreement.
  4. T3Sovereign Never leaves community systems. Withholding is itself sovereignty.

Governing Rules

  • Unclassified Indigenous material defaults to Sovereign (T3).
  • Moving data outward requires a documented human decision.
  • A derivative inherits its most restrictive input.

TSDF is v0.9.8, pre-release (revised 07/14/2026); version 1.0 comes only by ATNI resolution. This site describes a design goal, not a compliance claim.

TSDF Overview

Design Principles

  • 01

    Bring Your Own Data

    Tribal Nations retain exclusive custody of their data; TERRA delivers analytical engines and shared baseline layers, never a centralized repository.

  • 02

    Open Code, Protected Deploys

    Core frameworks remain open for peer review; Tribe-specific instances operate in isolated, secure environments governed by the Nation.

  • 03

    Open Window, Not Open Door

    Software releases are held under ATNI intellectual property stewardship; custom terms prevent external monetization without explicit Tribal consent.

  • 04

    Once It Works, It Keeps Working

    Standardized practices, automated pipelines, and thorough documentation ensure systems remain operational beyond any single person or funding cycle.
